The Club

Built from the ground up

Seme FC plays out of Seme, Machakos County, drawing players from the local community onto one of the area's dirt pitches for training and matchday. The badge — a shield split by a football and a gold ribbon — sits on the corner flags at every home fixture.

This page is the home of Seme FC. Fixtures and player squad details are maintained directly by the club management.
